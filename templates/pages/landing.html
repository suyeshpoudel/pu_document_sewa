Frequently asked questions

Where do I apply and pay?

On the official Pokhara University student portal at services.pu.edu.np. This site only collects your receipt and delivery details.

What receipt do I upload here?

The pickup receipt that is generated on your services.pu.edu.np application detail page once your college finishes processing — not the payment receipt. Include your PU application reference number as well.

How do I pay your fee?

Our flat pickup & delivery fee (Rs. {{ service_fee|default:500 }}) is collected in cash when your documents are delivered to your door. The PU fee itself is paid at services.pu.edu.np.

Can I order Transcript + Migration + Provisional together?

Yes — that's the TMP bundle on the PU portal (one application, one pickup receipt). Select the TMP bundle here and we'll collect all three for a single delivery fee.

How do I track my delivery?

Once dispatched, your application page shows the courier and tracking ID with a direct link to the courier's tracking page (e.g. Nepal Can Move).

How long does it take?

Collection usually starts right after your receipt is verified; delivery time then depends on the document's processing time at PU and your location. Track your application page for live status.
