How it works

Four clear steps, start to doorstep

You apply and pay on the official PU portal. We handle the campus pickup and courier delivery, and keep the status visible in your account.

Get started
1

Apply at the PU portal

Apply and pay the PU fee at services.pu.edu.np for a single certificate or the TMP bundle. Your college then processes the application.

2

Get your pickup receipt

When processing is complete, a pickup receipt appears on your PU application detail page. This is the document you upload here — not the payment receipt.

3

We collect from PU

Upload the pickup receipt with your delivery address. After we verify it, our team collects the certificates directly from the university.

4

Delivered to your door

We dispatch through a courier and show you the tracking details. Pay the confirmed service fee in cash when the documents arrive.

Before you apply

Have these ready before you start

A complete application avoids delays in verification and collection. The PU portal is the only place where the official university fee is paid.

  • A completed PU application at services.pu.edu.np, with the PU fee paid.
  • The pickup receipt generated on your PU application detail page once your college finishes processing.
  • Your PU application reference number, as shown next to the pickup receipt.
  • A complete delivery address, including municipality, ward, district, and province.
  • A phone number the courier can reach when your documents arrive.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where do I apply and pay the PU fee?
On the official Pokhara University student portal at services.pu.edu.np. This site only collects your pickup receipt and delivery details; it does not take the PU fee.
What receipt do I upload here?
Upload the pickup receipt that is generated on your services.pu.edu.np application detail page after your college finishes processing — not the payment receipt. Include your PU application reference number as well.
How do I pay the delivery fee?
Our pickup & delivery fee starts at Rs. {{ service_fee|default:500 }}. The final fee depends on the document, delivery location, and urgency, and is collected in cash by the courier when your documents are delivered. The PU fee itself is paid separately at services.pu.edu.np.
Can I order Transcript, Migration and Provisional together?
Yes. The TMP bundle on the PU portal is one application with one pickup receipt. Select the TMP bundle here and we will collect all three certificates for a single delivery fee.
How do I track my delivery?
Once your application is dispatched, your application page shows the courier, tracking ID, and a direct link to the courier's tracking page where available (for example, Nepal Can Move).
How long does it take?
Collection begins after we verify your pickup receipt. Delivery time then depends on the document's processing time at PU and your location. Your application page shows the latest status at every stage.
Can I cancel an application?
You can cancel while the application is still Submitted or Pickup Receipt Verified. Once collection or dispatch has started, cancellation is no longer available from your account.
